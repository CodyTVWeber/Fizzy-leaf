Our Story

Hello! My name is Christian Milford and I am the owner of Fizzy Leaf.

Tired of sugary drinks and artificial sweeteners, I started carbonating my mom’s hibiscus tea recipe in my kitchen. What began as an experiment is now the original sparkling tea — bold, tart, refreshing, and completely clean.

Every can is made with real brewed tea and sold in the communities where we live. We’re just getting started.
