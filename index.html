Our Story

Made the simple way.

Hello! My name is Christian Milford and I am the owner of Fizzy Leaf. Tired of sugary drinks and artificial sweeteners, I started carbonating my mom's hibiscus tea recipe in our kitchen right here in Middle Tennessee.

What began as a family tradition became something I knew I had to share. Today, Fizzy Leaf is proudly sold at 18 local shops across Tennessee — from Franklin to Nashville, Spring Hill to Columbia.

"The best flavors come straight from the earth. We go to great lengths to honor them — starting with real hibiscus tea and nothing artificial."
☕
Christian Milford
Founder, Fizzy Leaf Sparkling Tea
