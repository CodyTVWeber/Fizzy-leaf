📖 Meet the Founder

Brewed with Heart in Tennessee

Hello! My name is Christian Milford, and I am the founder of Fizzy Leaf. My journey started out of simple frustration. Tired of store shelves lined with overly sweetened drinks, chemical additives, and artificial stevia aftertastes, I wanted something pure, bubbly, and refreshing.

I went back to basics and started experimenting in my kitchen, carbonating my mother's signature hibiscus tea recipe. Steeping organic Roselle hibiscus flowers, balancing the natural tartness, and infusing it with perfectly calibrated carbonation—Fizzy Leaf was born.

"We don't cut corners. We brew the hard way with real ingredients, producing a clean sparkle you can feel good about drinking every day."

Today, we proudly brew and bottle Fizzy Leaf right here in Middle Tennessee, serving local coffee shops and neighbors who value transparency and real ingredients.
